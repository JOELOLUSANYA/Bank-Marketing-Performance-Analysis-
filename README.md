# Bank-Marketing-Performance-Analysis-
End to end business analytics case study analyzing bank marketing campaign data using SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS) and Power BI to optimize customer conversion strategies.
Bank Marketing Campaign Analytics & Performance Case Study
Project Overview
This project delivers a comprehensive business analytics case study examining direct telemarketing campaigns conducted by a financial institution. The primary objective is to analyze customer demographics, financial standing, and campaign attributes to uncover the core factors driving term deposit subscriptions (⁠deposit = 'yes'⁠), minimize resource waste, and improve future marketing conversion efficiency.
 Tech Stack & Tools
 Database & Querying: Microsoft SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)
 Data Transformation & Modeling: Power Query, DAX
 Data Visualization & Reporting: Power BI
  Key Performance Indicators & Baseline Metrics
 Total Clients Contacted: 45,211 phone calls analyzed.
 Total Subscriptions: 5,289 successful term deposit sign-ups.
 Overall Conversion Rate: ~11.7% efficiency baseline.
 Key Business Insights
1. Campaign Seasonality: Outreach volume and absolute success counts peak heavily during specific months (such as May), indicating clear seasonal patterns in customer responsiveness.
2. Demographic Concentration: Clients within specific age cohorts and educational tiers (such as secondary and tertiary levels) exhibit a much higher propensity to convert.
3. Historical Engagement: Previous campaign outcomes serve as a strong leading indicator for future conversions, suggesting that prioritizing past positive respondents increases efficiency.
Strategic Recommendations
 Hyper-Targeted Outreach: Reallocate marketing and telemarketing capacity toward high-performing demographic segments and job categories rather than relying on untargeted cold calling.
 Timing & Scheduling: Intensify campaign scheduling during high-yield seasonal windows (e.g., Q2 peaks) to maximize campaign return on investment.
 Lead Scoring Pipeline: Implement a prioritized callback list for clients with positive historical campaign outcomes.
